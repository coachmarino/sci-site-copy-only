What we measure

Rankings and Traffic Matter. Customers Matter More.

We still track rankings, organic traffic, Google Business Profile activity and website engagement, because they explain why results are improving or slipping. But the report that matters answers one question: is your marketing bringing in business?

  • Qualified leadsCalls, form submissions and inquiries from potential customers.
  • Booked opportunitiesAppointments, estimates, consultations and scheduled jobs.
  • Customer acquisitionHow marketing contributes to turning opportunities into customers.
  • Cost per leadWhat you spend to generate a qualified opportunity through paid and trackable marketing.
  • Sales and revenueRevenue generated by marketing, when it can be tracked accurately.
  • ROI and ROASWhat your business gets back from its marketing and advertising spend.

FAQ

Questions About Working With South Coast Inbound

What does South Coast Inbound do?

South Coast Inbound is a Southern New England digital marketing agency. We specialize in Local SEO, website design, Google Ads, social media marketing, email marketing and integrated digital marketing for small and medium-sized businesses. The work is aimed at helping you get found, generate qualified leads and turn more of those leads into customers.

Where does South Coast Inbound work?

We work with businesses throughout Massachusetts, Rhode Island and Connecticut, with a focus on Southern New England. That includes Providence, Newport, Fall River, New Bedford, New London and the surrounding communities. Our offices are in Swansea, MA, Warren, RI and Providence, RI.

Is South Coast Inbound only an SEO company?

No. Local SEO is one of our core specialties, but we also provide website design, Google Ads, social media marketing, email marketing, AI marketing and overall digital marketing strategy. Which channels we use depends on your business, your market and where the best growth opportunities are.

How do you measure marketing results?

By business outcomes. Depending on the campaign, that includes qualified leads, phone calls, booked opportunities, cost per lead, sales, revenue, ROI and ROAS. We also track rankings and traffic to understand what is changing. See our recent small business results.

What does digital marketing cost?

It depends on the services you need, your competition, your market, the marketing assets you already have and your growth goals. We take on both focused one-time projects and ongoing campaigns, and we explain pricing before you start.

What happens after I schedule a consultation?

We talk about your business, your current marketing, your customers, your goals and the problems you are trying to solve. From there we identify the strongest opportunities and decide together whether South Coast Inbound is a good fit.

Do you require long-term contracts?

Not always. Terms depend on the service and scope. Some projects are one-time engagements, and ongoing services may have different terms depending on the campaign and what we manage. We explain the commitment, pricing and expectations before you start, so there are no surprises.
